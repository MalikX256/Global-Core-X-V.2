Enable GlobalCoreX Permissions

GlobalCoreX works best with a couple of permissions from your browser. You're always in control — these can be changed anytime in your browser or phone settings.

Location Access Required
Powers Live GPS, the map, route planning and SOS alerts.
Notifications Recommended
Confirms on your device when an SOS alert has gone out.
Background & Storage Access Recommended
Keeps tracking running while the screen is on and protects your saved locations, contacts and devices from being cleared by the browser.
Location is blocked in your browser. Open your browser's site settings (tap the lock/info icon next to the address bar) and set Location to "Allow", then reload this page.
Browsers don't allow web apps true unlimited background execution or OS admin rights — this is a security boundary, not a GlobalCoreX limit. What's above is the strongest real equivalent the Web platform allows. For always-on native background tracking, GlobalCoreX would need to ship as a native/PWA-installed app.
